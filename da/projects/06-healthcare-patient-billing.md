# Healthcare Patient and Billing Dashboard

**Evidence:** Screenshot: `Screenshot 2026-11-03 183159.png`

## What the evidence shows

The dashboard shows 55.50K total patients and 25.54K average billing. It includes admissions by year (2019–2024), counts by insurance provider (Cigna, Medicare, UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross, Aetna), patient gender split, and billing by medical condition (Diabetes, Arthritis, Obesity, Hypertension, Asthma, Cancer).

## Unknowns and evidence limits

The screenshot does not show the source system, currency, definition of patient count, billing period, whether values are billed or paid amounts, or privacy controls. The medical-condition billing values are not clinical outcomes.

## Tools and implementation evidence

Dashboard visualization is evident; tool not confirmed.

## Recommended technical validation and improvements

Protect patient privacy; aggregate and suppress small groups; define billing versus reimbursement; validate admission-year coverage; do not infer health outcomes or treatment effectiveness from billing amounts.
